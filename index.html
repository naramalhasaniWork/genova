How we operate

Assess→ Market & regulatory assessment Product-level assessment before anything moves: demand measured against Iraq's shortage lists, the registration route, channel fit, and commercial risk. A written go / no-go — not a hunch. Register→ MoH pathway · scientific-office framework Registration prepared for Iraq's licensed scientific-office framework — CPP and GMP dossiers assembled to the standard FDA- and EMA-credentialed manufacturers audit against.
